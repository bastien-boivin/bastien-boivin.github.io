# OZCAR TERENO 2025

📍 **Location:** Paris, France  
📅 **Dates:** September 29 – October 2, 2025  

---

## Presentation

> **Title:** Water Balance of a Managed Reservoir under Climate Change: Complementary Process-Based and Lumped Conceptual Modeling
> **Authors:** **B. Boivin**, A. Coche, R. Abhervé, R. Guillossou, J.-Y. Gaubert, L. Aquilina, J.-R. De Dreuzy  
> **Type:** Poster communication (abstract accepted)  

---

## Abstract

This work presents a coupled approach between hydrological and hydrogeological models to optimize the predictive management of drinking-water reservoirs under climate change.  
The case study focuses on the **Chèze dam (Brittany, France)**, a strategic resource supplying nearly 500,000 inhabitants of the Rennes metropolitan area...
